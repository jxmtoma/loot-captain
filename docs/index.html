What it does

Fast, slot-aware comparisons

Local profiles

Create multiple characters or import worn gear from RaidLoot and EverQuest /output inventory files.

Useful scoring

Choose a formula and compare upgrades, downgrades, and individual stat changes—including paired ears, wrists, and fingers.

Two supported sites

See compact badges and expandable stat differences on RaidLoot and OpenDKP item pages.

Character management

See every slot at a glance.

Arrange EverQuest equipment around the character and inspect one selected item on the right without scrolling through the entire inventory.

How it works

Three steps, no account

  1. 1
    Add a character

    Create a profile or import existing equipment.

  2. 2
    Choose a formula

    Select how Loot Captain should value stats.

  3. 3
    Browse gear

    Open RaidLoot or OpenDKP and review the comparison badge.

Local-first by design

Your profiles stay in your browser.

Loot Captain has no account, analytics, advertising, or tracking. Public RaidLoot pages are requested only when an import or missing-stat lookup needs them.
