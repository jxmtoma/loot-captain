Feature tour

From character setup to loot night.

Everything in Loot Captain works together as one local gear-planning flow.

01 / 08

Build your crew

Create, edit, delete, and switch between multiple local characters.

  • Keep name, class, level, equipment, and wishlist together.
  • Switch the active character from the popup or profile editor.
02 / 08

Import gear in seconds

Start from a public RaidLoot profile or an EverQuest /output inventory file.

  • Import worn equipment and augments, then edit anything manually.
  • Fetch missing stats and icons now or automatically when comparing.
  • Use built-in diagnostics when a public item cannot be resolved.
03 / 08

See the whole character

Review worn gear in a familiar EverQuest-style 19-slot layout.

  • Inspect paired ears, wrists, and fingers separately.
  • Move between equipment, augments, and spell focus or proc views.
  • Open item details without hunting through a long inventory list.
04 / 08

Know the real upgrade

See a compact verdict first, then expand the exact worn, candidate, and delta values.

  • Choose from eleven score formulas for defenses, pools, heroics, and regeneration.
  • Respect class eligibility, paired slots, empty slots, and weapon layouts.
  • Compare compatible augments and weapon Damage/Delay ratio.
05 / 08

Compare more than raw stats

Loot Captain keeps important named effects visible instead of hiding them inside one score.

  • See spell focus effects as added, removed, covered, or changed in potency.
  • Compare weapon procs by identity and damage direction.
  • Open dedicated focus and proc detail panels.
06 / 08

Turn browsing into a loot plan

Star items into a wishlist that belongs to the active character.

  • Highlight wanted items on RaidLoot and in live OpenDKP auctions.
  • Compare a candidate with equipped gear or a compatible wishlist baseline.
  • Match the same item across both sites and enrich missing details later.
07 / 08

Works where loot decisions happen

Compare items without leaving the RaidLoot or OpenDKP page you are already using.

  • Annotate RaidLoot item pages, search rows, stats, and wishlist cards.
  • Cover OpenDKP item details, tables, live auctions, and hover tooltips.
  • Refresh annotations when the active profile or formula changes.
08 / 08

Local-first by design

Your profiles, settings, cached item stats, and wishlists stay in your browser.

  • No Loot Captain account, analytics, advertising, or tracking.
  • No authenticated cookies or remotely hosted code.
  • Public RaidLoot lookups run only when imports or missing item data need them.
